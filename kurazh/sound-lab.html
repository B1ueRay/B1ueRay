Sound Lab

Every Kurazh sound, synthesized live in your browser. Call it or do it — then listen.

Sounds are a second channel, never the only one: every cue here has a visible counterpart in the app, and on phones it is paired with a haptic tap. API: KurazhSound.play('win', { volume, delay }). Mute is stored in localStorage["kurazh.sound"].
