5. How dares work

  1. Taker settings. Before or during a stream, the Taker chooses: a minimum amount per dare, a minimum viewer age (18+ or 21+), categories, the dare types they accept, and the number of free passes for the stream (3 unless the Taker changes it).
  2. Writing a dare. A Caller writes a short dare in their own words, picks a type and backs it with at least the Taker’s minimum. Every dare is checked by our safety systems before it is shown. Dares that break the Community Guidelines are blocked or removed.
  3. Backing. Other Callers can add money to a dare’s pot. The pot is the total of all holds on that dare.
  4. The Taker decides. The Taker can accept a dare, decline it (see section 8) or let it wait. Dares are paused while a Taker is driving or on a break, and may be paused for safety reasons (for example during an air-raid alert).
  5. Doing the dare. If the Taker accepts, they try the dare within the time shown. They can stop at any moment.
  6. Voting. When the time ends or the Taker says it is done, eligible viewers vote on whether it counts (section 7).

6. Holds, charges and releases

6.1 Holds

When you back a dare, you authorise our payment partner to place an authorisation hold on your card for that amount. A hold is not a charge: the money stays in your account but your bank may show it as “pending” and you may not be able to spend it while it is held. You may be asked to confirm the hold with your bank (for example 3-D Secure).

6.2 When a hold is charged

Your hold for a dare is charged (captured) only if the Taker completed the dare and at least 60% of eligible votes say it counts. The charge is made by [Payment service provider] and appears on your statement as [statement descriptor].

6.3 When a hold is released

Your hold is released in full if the dare is declined for any reason, the Taker gives up, time runs out, the vote does not reach 60%, the stream ends before the dare is taken, the dare is removed by moderation, or we stop the stream. Holds that are not taken by the end of a stream are released automatically. We tell our payment partner to release the hold straight away, but your bank decides when the funds show as available again; this can take several days.

6.4 Limits

To protect you and others we apply limits, for example a daily hold limit (currently [$500], lower for new accounts), a maximum pot per dare and a limit on cards per account. We may refuse or cancel a hold that looks fraudulent or breaks these Terms.

7. Voting

  • Only eligible voters can vote: verified accounts that watched enough of the dare live, as measured by our servers. The Taker and accounts linked to the Taker cannot vote on the Taker’s dares. Other criteria may apply and are shown in the app.
  • One vote per verified person per dare. Using bots, multiple accounts, paid voting or coordinated voting is forbidden and may lead to the result being reversed and accounts being banned.
  • The result is calculated on our servers. We may review a result if it is close, if the pot is large, or if we detect manipulation, and we may change it after review. If we change a result, holds are captured or released to match the corrected result, within the hold’s validity.
  • Vote by what you saw. “Counts” means the Taker did what the dare said, within the time and the rules.

8.2 What counts as a pass

Giving up on a dare, letting the time run out, and leaving the top eligible dare unanswered for more than 2 minutes count as a pass, unless you chose one of the always-free reasons above. Time does not run while dares are paused (driving, break, safety pause).

8.3 Decline fees

After your free passes for a stream are used up, each extra pass has a decline fee of 10% of that dare’s pot, at least [$1] and at most $20. The fee:

  • is taken only from your earnings from the same stream and never exceeds them. If you have no earnings from that stream, no fee is charged. Your balance never goes below zero and you never owe Kurazh a debt for declining;
  • is never charged for the always-free reasons in 8.1, and is never charged for any refusal based on safety, health, consent or the law;
  • is shared among that dare’s backers in proportion to their holds, as a “decline bonus”, in the way described in the app [credited as / paid to];
  • is shown to you before you go live and before each paid pass.

We review use of the “Unsafe” reason to improve safety. We will not charge decline fees retroactively because of a review. If someone repeatedly misuses the reason, we may contact them or apply the Community Guidelines.

8.4 Free passes

Free passes reset at the start of each stream and cannot be carried over, sold or transferred. A Taker can set more free passes than the default.
