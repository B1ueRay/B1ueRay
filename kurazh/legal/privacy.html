Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and what you control.

Version [0.1-draft] · Effective date: [Effective date] · Last updated: [Date]

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run live dares safely: your account, verified identity, payments, streams, chat and votes.
  • Your ID document and selfie are checked by our verification provider. We do not store the images ourselves; we get the result.
  • We never see your full card number. Our payment partner handles it.
  • With Google sign-in we get only your Google account ID, email, name and profile picture.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.
  • You can see, download, correct and delete your data. Some records must be kept by law.

3. Google sign-in

If you choose “Continue with Google”, Google shares with us your Google account identifier, email address and whether it is verified, your name and your profile picture. We ask only for the basic openid, email and profile permissions. We do not get your Google password, contacts, files or other Google data, and we do not post anything to your Google account.

We verify the sign-in token on our servers and use it only to create and secure your account. Signing in with Google does not replace phone or identity verification. Google’s handling of your data is covered by Google’s privacy policy. You can disconnect Kurazh in your Google account settings; you will then need another way to sign in.

4. Identity verification

Before money moves, before you go live and before you enter the 18+ section, we need to confirm that you are a real adult and who you are. Our provider [KYC provider] asks for a photo of your ID document and a short live selfie, compares them, checks the document and screens your name against sanctions and politically-exposed-person lists.

  • The selfie comparison uses biometric data. It is processed by the provider only for this check, on the basis of your explicit consent [or the legal obligation identified by counsel].
  • We receive the result and the details listed in section 2, not the images. The provider keeps the images for [period] under our contract with them.
  • In the 18+ section, every person who appears on camera is verified in the same way, and their consent records are kept.

5. Payments and payouts

Card details are entered in a secure form run by [Payment service provider]. We never receive or store your full card number or security code. The provider may also act as an independent controller for its own legal duties, such as fraud prevention and anti-money-laundering checks.

For Takers we process earnings and payout details to pay you, keep accounting records, and meet tax and anti-money-laundering obligations, including reporting to tax authorities where the law requires.

6. Streams, chat and safety

  • Live streams are shown to viewers in real time. Other users can see your nickname, profile picture, stream, public chat messages, dares you write and your Courage score.
  • To keep people safe, our automated systems analyse dares, chat, and samples of video and audio from live streams, and human moderators can watch streams. This helps us find dangerous acts, minors, nudity outside the 18+ section, threats, self-harm and personal data.
  • We keep short clips of each dare and the voting record so we can handle disputes and chargebacks.
  • Streams are routed through our media servers, so other users do not see your IP address.
  • If someone’s life or safety is at immediate risk, we may share necessary information (for example the stream and approximate location) with emergency services.

7. Analytics, cookies and local storage

  • Strictly necessary: we store a session cookie and a few settings (such as sound, language and your consent choice) on your device. These are needed for the Service to work.
  • Analytics (optional): with your consent we record how the app is used (screens opened, features used, errors) without your name, email, phone or the text of your dares and chat. You can change your choice in Profile at any time. We respect “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or sell data to advertisers.
  • The app loads fonts from [Google Fonts / our own servers].

9. Automated decisions

Some decisions are made or prepared automatically: the safety filter can block a dare or message, fraud checks can refuse a hold or delay a payout, and moderation tools can pause a stream. Where a decision has a significant effect on you, a person reviews it on request, and you can explain your side and appeal (see section 14 of the Terms). Exact matches with known child sexual abuse material lead to immediate action and are reported as the law requires.

  • Other users: your public profile, streams, chat, dares and Courage score.
  • Service providers who work for us under contract: hosting ([hosting provider]), media servers ([streaming provider]), payments ([Payment service provider]), identity verification ([KYC provider]), SMS and email ([messaging provider]), content moderation ([moderation provider]), customer support ([support tool]).

We do not sell personal data.

13. Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, strong password hashing, two-step verification and passkeys, access controls and logging for staff, regular security testing and a responsible disclosure programme. If a breach is likely to put you at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay. Report security issues to [security email].

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and the Law of Ukraine “On Personal Data Protection”), you can:

  • get a copy of your data and information about how we use it;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data (Profile → Security → Delete account), except data we must keep by law;
  • restrict or object to some uses, including uses based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • ask for a person to review an automated decision.

Email [privacy email]. We reply within one month. We may need to confirm your identity first.

15. Children

Kurazh is only for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that a child is using Kurazh or appears in a stream, we close the account and delete the data, except what we must keep to protect the child and to report to the authorities. If you believe a child is on Kurazh, report it in the app or at [safety email].
